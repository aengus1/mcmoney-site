Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

In short: McMoney stores your household's expenses so the people in it can share them. It doesn't show ads, doesn't track you, doesn't use analytics, and doesn't sell or share your data with anyone. You can delete your account in the app at any time.

Who we are

McMoney is made by Aengus McCullough, an independent developer in Canada. Questions about this policy: mcmoneydev@proton.me.

What McMoney stores

  • Your sign-in. You sign in with Apple. McMoney receives an anonymous identifier from Apple for your Apple ID. It does not ask Apple for your email address.
  • Your name, as you type it when creating or joining a household, so the others can see who entered each expense. Apple may offer your first name to fill this in; it stays on your iPhone unless you use it.
  • Your household's data: its name, the people in it, and the expenses, categories, accounts and budgets you enter, including any notes. Account names are whatever you type (for example "Visa"); McMoney never asks for card or bank numbers and never connects to your bank.
  • Invite codes you create, until they're used or expire.

Who can see it

Only the people in your household, when signed in. The server enforces this for every request. People join a household only with an invite code from someone already in it.

Where it's kept

Your household's data is stored with Supabase, which hosts McMoney's database on Amazon Web Services in the United States (Oregon). A copy is also kept on your iPhone so the app works offline. Data is encrypted in transit between the app and the server. Sign in with Apple is provided by Apple.

These are the only services McMoney uses. Your data is not shared with anyone else, is not used for advertising, and is not sold.

How long it's kept

Your data is kept while your account exists. When you delete an expense, it's marked deleted and hidden everywhere, but kept in the household's records (so a phone that was offline can't bring it back) until the household is deleted.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Manage › Delete Account. This deletes your sign-in and your name, removes you from your household, and cancels McMoney's access to your Apple ID.

  • If others are still in the household, the expenses you entered stay for them (shown as entered by "Someone else"), because they're part of the household's shared records.
  • If you're the last person in the household, the household and everything in it is deleted.

Deleted data may remain in the hosting provider's backups for a limited time before it's overwritten. Signing out instead removes the household's data from your iPhone but keeps it on the server.

Your rights

You can see and change your household's data in the app at any time, and delete your account as described above. For anything else, such as a copy of your data, email mcmoneydev@proton.me.

Children

McMoney is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
