Support

McMoney is a quick way for a household to track its spending together on iPhone: enter an expense in a few taps, see where the money goes, and keep an eye on monthly budgets.

Need help or found a problem?
Email mcmoneydev@proton.me. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened.

Common questions

How do I add someone to my household?

Go to Manage › Household › Invite Someone and share the code. They install McMoney, sign in, tap Join with a Code and enter it. Each code works once and lasts 7 days.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, for expenses: add, edit and delete them offline and they upload when you're back online. The Expenses tab shows anything still waiting. Changing categories, accounts and budgets needs a connection.

How do refunds work?

Switch the entry screen from Expense to Refund. A refund reduces that category's spending.

Can I bring in my old spreadsheet?

Yes. Manage › Import Expenses reads a CSV file with the columns date,amount,category,subcategory,account,person,note (dates as year-month-day). Set up your categories and accounts first; the importer lists any names it doesn't recognise and imports nothing until the file is clean.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Manage › Delete Account. If others are still in your household, the expenses you entered stay for them; if you're the last person, the whole household is deleted. See the Privacy Policy for details.
